# About OWASP New Zealand

The [OWASP New Zealand Chapter](https://owasp.org/www-chapter-new-zealand/) has been active since 2007. In addition to supporting and participating in the annual [OWASP New Zealand Day](https://appsec.org.nz/conference) conference, we organise local Meetups and training events throughout the year.

The Chapter's leaders are:

* [Matt Cotterell](mailto:matt.cotterell@owasp.org) - Wellington    
* [Kirk Jackson](mailto:kirk.jackson@owasp.org) - Wellington

## OWASP New Zealand Meetups

### Auckland

Auckland Meetups are held at 6:00 p.m. on the second Tuesdays of March, May, July, September, and November.

For information on upcoming events in Auckland, visit the [Auckland Meetup's](https://www.meetup.com/OWASP-New-Zealand-Chapter-Auckland) page.

### Hamilton

Hamilton Meetups are held at 6:00 p.m. on the third Tuesdays of March, May, July, September, and November. Our default Meetup location is
at Gallagher's World Headquarters, 181 Kahikatea Drive.

For information on upcoming events in Hamilton, visit the [Hamilton Meetup's](https://www.meetup.com/owaspnz-hamilton/) page.

### Wellington

Wellington Meetups are currently being scheduled on an *ad hoc* basis.

For information on upcoming events in Wellington, visit the [Wellington Meetup's](https://www.meetup.com/OWASP-Wellington) page.

### Christchurch

Christchurch Meetups are currently being scheduled on an *ad hoc* basis.

For information on upcoming events in Christchurch, visit the [Christchurch Meetup's](https://www.meetup.com/OWASP-New-Zealand-Chapter-Christchurch) page.
