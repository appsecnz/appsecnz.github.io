## Course Description

Most people who have ever experienced a threat model, remember a painstaking long workshop that results in so much work 
to do, it becomes overwhelming. This leads to no-one ever wanting to do a threat model again and loses its value if no 
actions can actually be committed to or implemented.

Participants learn how to quickly defi ne scope, identify critical assets, enumerate realistic threat scenarios, and 
assess impact - all within a strict timebox, with the focus on speed, clarity, and decision-making - without becoming 
an academic exercise in finding every possible threat or solving for cosmic radiation.

The lightweight, repeatable threat-modelling approach taught is ideal for development teams, security teams, architects, 
product owners, technical leaders, and anyone involved in system design or change. The methodology is usable by 
mixed-discipline teams and scalable across the organisation, drawing on well-known established concepts but distils them 
into a format that works in real-world environments.

The training session will cover:   
* A threat modelling methodology you can use immediately and complete in 15 minutes
* A shared vocabulary for discussing threats with non-technical stakeholders
* The ability to surface meaningful security risks and mitigations early
* How to avoid analysis paralysis and prioritise decision-making
* Confidence to run quick threat-modelling sessions independently or within teams
* Suitable for all levels - no prior threat-modelling experience is required

This session is the second in our four-module course. By this point, participants have felt what it is like to be in an 
incident and learned skills of how to respond. This session teaches them how to triage quickly with threat models of what 
would be critical to an organisation and gets them ready for the following sessions.
